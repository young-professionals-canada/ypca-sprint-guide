05

Review before publishing

AI creates the first draft. You are responsible for accuracy, clarity, and what becomes public.

My identity is clearSomeone can understand my field and direction in five seconds.
My strongest work is easy to findProjects, research, cases, or experience appear near the top.
The writing sounds like meThe language is direct, natural, and not filled with buzzwords.
Every claim is accurateNo invented achievements, employers, projects, or statistics.
Every link worksResume, LinkedIn, email, projects, and navigation have been tested.
The mobile version worksText is readable and nothing overlaps on a phone.
Private information is removedNo home address, student number, or unwanted phone number.
Important: a polished design cannot compensate for inaccurate information.
06

Publish your website

Publish from v0

Get the public link first. That is enough to complete the workshop.

  1. Open the Publish menu in your v0 project.
  2. Publish the production version.
  3. Open the public link and test it.
  4. Use Publish Changes after future edits.
Official v0 guide ↗

Connect a custom domain

This is optional and can be completed after the workshop.

  1. Open the related Vercel project.
  2. Go to Settings → Domains.
  3. Enter the domain you own.
  4. Copy the DNS records Vercel displays into your domain provider.
  5. Wait for Vercel to verify the configuration.
Official domain guide ↗
Your domain can stay with its current provider. You normally connect it using the DNS values shown by Vercel. Domain registration itself may cost money.
