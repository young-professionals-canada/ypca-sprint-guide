## Evidence should be easy to inspect

Use the profile sections available to your account to show projects, volunteering, courses, licenses, certifications, languages, honors, publications, or other relevant evidence. Availability and placement can vary by account and subscription, so focus on the sections you can support and maintain.

Add a work sample only when it is safe to publish and helps the visitor understand your contribution. Useful proof can include a portfolio page, case study, report, presentation, article, project repository, event recap, research poster, or public dashboard.

Give the item a clear title and description. Explain the problem, your contribution, and what the visitor will see. Never upload confidential material, private participant information, internal employer documents, or work you do not have permission to share.

## Create a stable profile address

LinkedIn lets you customize the public part of your profile URL. Use a recognizable variation of your name or professional identity, avoiding unnecessary numbers when a clear option is available. Test the link in a private browsing window before placing it on a resume, website, application, or email signature.

Do not change the address repeatedly. An old link may remain associated with you temporarily, but frequent changes make your profile harder to find and can break materials you already shared.

## Choose Open to Work visibility deliberately

LinkedIn currently supports different Open to Work visibility choices, including all members, recruiters only, and visible only to you. Sharing with all members may add a profile-photo frame. Recruiters-only visibility is less public, but LinkedIn says it cannot guarantee complete privacy from recruiters connected to your current employer.

Choose accurate job titles, locations, location types, employment types, and start-date preferences. Broad or conflicting selections can make your direction harder to understand.

> Visibility is not automatically better when it is wider. Choose the setting that supports your search while respecting your current situation.
