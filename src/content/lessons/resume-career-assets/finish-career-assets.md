## Make the system reusable

The value of this course is not one application. It is the system you can repeat: update evidence, select what matters, tailor honestly, save the final version, and learn from the result.

Use filenames that remain understandable later. For example:

- `Master-Profile-2026-08.md`
- `Resume-Base-2026-08.docx`
- `Resume-YPCA-Program-Coordinator-2026-08-24.pdf`
- `Project-Brief-Transit-Delay-Dashboard.md`

Keep editable files and submission files. Word or source files let you revise; PDF files preserve the version you actually sent.

## Run the five-minute quality check

Before submitting anything, confirm:

- the employer and role name are correct;
- every claim is supported by your Master Profile;
- contact information and links work;
- formatting remains readable at 100% zoom;
- the filename is professional and specific;
- the submitted file is the version you intended.

## Keep improving the evidence

After a project, course, volunteer role, or interview, update the Master Profile while details are fresh. Add what you did, tools used, outcomes, feedback, and anything you learned not to claim.

> You now have a source of truth, a reusable resume, a tailoring workflow, and a project plan. The next course turns those assets into a public website.
